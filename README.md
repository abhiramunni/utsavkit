# UtsavKit — Your Indian Festival Year, Planned Ahead

Never miss a festival again. UtsavKit holds every major Indian festival from today (Karthigai Deepam, 24 November 2026) to Christmas 2027 — with real dates, countdowns, a circular year wheel, prep checklists for each festival, and every long weekend of 2027 worth booking travel for.

**Live:** https://abhiramunni.github.io/utsavkit/

## What it does

- **Today card** — what is happening today (Karthigai Deepam *and* Guru Nanak Jayanti on 24 Nov 2026) and a live countdown to the next festival.
- **Festival wheel** — one circle covering November 2026 to October 2027. Every dot is a festival on its date; tap any dot to open it. Your own occasions appear in terracotta.
- **Coming up** — the next six festivals, nearest first, with days-to-go rings and prep progress.
- **Browse the year** — month-by-month festival list (November 2026 to December 2027), plus a starred-only view.
- **Long weekends of 2027** — six stretches worth booking trains for, with the leave day to take.
- **Festival detail sheets** — date, region, community, what the festival is, and a tickable prep list (saved on your device).
- **Your occasions** — add birthdays and anniversaries; they appear on the wheel and in the month list.
- **Six plain-language guides** — why dates move, panchang vs solar vs moon dates, moon-sighting, festival travel, gifts, fasting.
- **Your data stays yours** — everything is saved in localStorage only. Copy a festival summary, remove the sample occasions, or erase everything with two taps.

## Dates — verified 24 November 2026

- 2027 dates follow the central government holiday list (DoPT, 16 July 2026) and standard panchang calculations.
- Karthigai Deepam and Guru Nanak Jayanti are both 24 November 2026; Christmas is 25 December 2026.
- Holi colours fall on Monday 22 March 2027; the government holiday is Tuesday 23 March. Makar Sankranti is 15 January by most panchangs (government list: 14 January).
- Moon-sighted dates (Eid ul-Fitr 10 March, Eid ul-Zuha 17 May 2027) are expected dates and can move by a day. Regional observance can shift a date by a day — the app says so wherever it matters.
